# 🍮 V & G LecheFlan

### Web-Based Dessert Shop Sales and Management System

## 📌 Project Overview

The **V & G LecheFlan Web-Based Dessert Shop Sales and Management System** is designed to improve the daily operations of V & G LecheFlan, a dessert shop specializing in leche flan and other desserts.

The system integrates point-of-sale transactions, online ordering, delivery management, inventory monitoring, automated reporting, and Llama AI-assisted features into a centralized platform for efficient business management.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Develop a **POS module** to centralize in-store and online transactions, orders, and pre-orders.
- Develop an **Online Ordering module** for accurate order processing and pre-order scheduling.
- Design a **Delivery Management module** for delivery details, order tracking, and delivery assignments.
- Develop an **Automated Reporting module** for daily, weekly, and monthly sales and inventory reports.
- Develop an **Inventory Management module** for stock updates, expiration monitoring, and low-stock alerts.
- Integrate **Llama AI** for dessert recommendations, calorie estimation, and raw-material-based production predictions.
- Evaluate system quality using **ISO/IEC 25010** criteria: functionality, usability, reliability, performance efficiency, security, and maintainability.

## ✨ Features

### 🧾 1. Point-of-Sale (POS) Module

- Record in-store and online transactions.
- Centralize orders, pre-orders, and sales records.
- Record customer and transaction information.
- Calculate transaction totals and applicable discounts.
- Update inventory after completed transactions.

### 🛍️ 2. Online Ordering Module

- Allow customers to browse available desserts and product details.
- Enable customers to place online orders.
- Support scheduled pre-orders.
- Display product prices and availability.
- Collect customer and order information.
- Provide order confirmation and order status updates.

### 🚚 3. Delivery Management Module

- Allow customers to enter delivery details.
- Enable customers to track order status.
- Allow authorized staff to manage delivery schedules.
- Organize delivery assignments.
- Coordinate order processing and delivery information.

### 📊 4. Automated Reporting Module

- Generate daily sales and inventory reports.
- Generate weekly sales and inventory reports.
- Generate monthly sales and inventory reports.
- Summarize sales trends and revenue.
- Monitor product performance.
- Present report data to authorized staff and administrators for business decision-making.

### 📦 5. Inventory Management Module

- Automatically update stock levels after qualifying transactions.
- Monitor product availability and expiration dates.
- Display low-stock notifications.
- Record stock-in and stock-out transactions.
- Track raw materials used for production.
- Provide inventory information for production planning.
- Support AI-assisted estimates of potential production quantities.

### 🤖 6. Llama AI Integration

**Browse Product module**
- Recommend desserts based on customer preferences.
- Provide estimated calorie information for desserts.

**Inventory module**
- Analyze available raw materials.
- Predict potential production quantities based on available ingredients.
- Support inventory and production planning.

*AI-generated recommendations, calorie estimates, and production predictions depend on the accuracy of the available data and the capabilities of the configured AI service.*

### ✅ 7. System Quality Evaluation

The system will be evaluated using **ISO/IEC 25010** software quality criteria:

- **Functional suitability:** Checks whether system functions meet the specified requirements.
- **Usability:** Evaluates ease of use and navigation.
- **Reliability:** Assesses consistent and dependable operation.
- **Performance efficiency:** Evaluates response times and resource usage.
- **Security:** Assesses access control and protection of information.
- **Maintainability:** Evaluates how easily the system can be modified and maintained.
